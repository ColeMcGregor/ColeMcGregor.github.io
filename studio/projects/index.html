Development Philosophy

Software Built to Accomplish a Purpose

Wise Raven Studios is not limited to one type of software. Projects may be educational, practical, experimental, playful, or some combination of all four.

What connects them is a focus on practical and helpful systems, clear design, meaningful interaction, and software that improves the experience of the people using it.
