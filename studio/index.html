About the Studio

Well-designed software with a clear purpose.

Wise Raven Studios develops software intended to make learning, working, and playing more engaging. Projects are mostly oriented toward whatever great idea has been had recently, and how well it can affect and improve the lives of those who use it.

Each project is approached with an emphasis on maintainability, accessibility, clear design, and the needs of the people who will actually use it. Additionally, Wise Raven Studios is committed to working with collaborators who share its values and can contribute to the development of accessible, useful, ethical, conscientious, and, of course, enjoyable software experiences.
