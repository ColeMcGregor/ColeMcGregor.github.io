01 / About Me

A little about how I have come to be a software systems engineer.

My background consists of formal software development education, professional contract software engineering work, and military service. I joined the U.S. Air Force after high school, where I served as an Aircraft Armament Systems Specialist, and learned to work within complex, technical systems requiring rigorous situational awareness and attention to detail.

As my time in service ended, I turned to software and game development, learning my passion for programming, and subsequently systems design and architecture as I sought to understand how to better build software. I learned that supplementing my education with independent study, reading, and experimentation was essential in developing and improving my understanding of software and the systems around them.

Outside of professional development, I spend time thinking up and creating games, reading, gardening, and studying new subjects that help me understand both technology and the people who use it. I keep a list of relevant books, courses, and ideas in my learning journal.
